About me

I am a clinical data and medical imaging AI researcher based in Brisbane, Australia. My work brings together clinical records, structural and diffusion MRI, quantitative CT and machine learning to investigate neurological disease, occupational lung disease and cancer. I work with clinical and research teams across study design, data preparation, method development and evaluation.

Career & education

I completed my PhD in machine learning and neuroimaging at The University of Queensland in 2024, supervised by Professor David Reutens and Associate Professor Viktor Vegh. My doctoral research focused on MRI and machine learning in epilepsy.

My previous research appointments include QIMR Berghofer’s Cerebellum & Neurodegeneration Research Group, UQ’s Centre for Advanced Imaging, and a visiting research role with RBWH Neurology. Earlier research experience at IIIT Sri City and the University of Hyderabad established my foundations in machine learning and feature selection.

Education: PhD, The University of Queensland (2024); M.Tech in Information Technology, University of Hyderabad (2016); MCA (2013) and BCA (2010), West Bengal University of Technology.

Selected recognition & service

PReMI–Springer Student Award (2017) · MICCAI Student Participation Award (2021) · UQ HDR travel support to attend AES (2023).

Academic service includes reviewing for ML4H and programme committee contributions to the ICCV CVAMD workshop.
