Research

Imaging methods.
Clinical questions.

My research brings together medical imaging AI, quantitative biomarkers and clinical evaluation. I work across structural and diffusion MRI and thoracic CT, connecting methodological development with questions in diagnosis, disease characterisation and clinical translation.

Neuroimaging & neurological disease

Epilepsy and focal cortical dysplasia

Developing MRI-based approaches to lesion detection and segmentation, including multimodal T1w/FLAIR learning and unsupervised analysis of ultra-high-field MRI. The NeuroMorphix programme investigates brain asymmetry and clinical information for seizure recurrence prediction after a first unprovoked seizure.

Neurodegeneration and diffusion MRI

Automated cerebellar peduncle segmentation and quantitative diffusion MRI in Friedreich’s ataxia, alongside morphometric and network-based analyses of Alzheimer’s disease. This work aims to make imaging measurements more reproducible and sensitive to disease-related variation.

Occupational lung disease & thoracic imaging

Imaging in dust-exposed workers

At I-MED Radiology Network, I lead research delivery and analysis in occupational lung imaging. ExactDust examines agreement and diagnostic performance between chest radiography and HRCT. Related work focuses on quantitative HRCT assessment of structural lung abnormalities.

Connecting lung structure and function

My ACARP-supported research evaluates CT-derived measures of lung function against spirometry and other available pulmonary function measures in coal mine dust lung disease.

Clinical AI evaluation & translation

Across these projects, I focus on validation in clinical cohorts, multi-site variability, harmonisation, error analysis and transparent reporting. My experience spans clinical data curation, imaging quality control, segmentation, statistical analysis and reproducible machine learning pipelines.

Methods: MRI and CT analysis · Deep learning · Quantitative biomarkers · Multi-site harmonisation · Diagnostic performance evaluation
