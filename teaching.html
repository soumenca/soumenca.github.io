Research supervision

I supervise postgraduate and undergraduate research projects in medical imaging AI. Current supervision includes B.Tech Computer Science students developing a shared brain MRI lesion segmentation pipeline.

Projects emphasise data quality, preprocessing, reproducible training, meaningful baselines, lesion-level evaluation and failure analysis.

Potential project areas

  • Brain MRI lesion detection and segmentation
  • Structural and diffusion MRI biomarkers
  • Quantitative thoracic imaging
  • Robustness and clinical evaluation of imaging AI
