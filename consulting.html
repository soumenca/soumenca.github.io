Consulting & Training

I welcome enquiries from researchers, industry teams and educators looking for support with medical imaging AI, clinical AI evaluation and practical training.

For researchers and research teams

Research consulting

Support with turning an imaging research question into a clear analysis and evaluation plan.

  • Study design and analysis planning
  • MRI and CT analysis, segmentation and quantitative biomarkers
  • Reproducible workflows and interpretation of results

For teams developing or evaluating imaging AI

Industry support

Research and evaluation support to understand model performance, limitations and evidence needs.

  • Imaging data quality and evaluation planning
  • Model validation, error analysis and performance reporting
  • Assessment across datasets and imaging sites

For educators and teams building clinical AI skills

Teaching & workshops

Enquiries are welcome for guest teaching, practical workshops and training shaped around your audience and learning goals.

  • Medical imaging and machine learning foundations
  • Critical appraisal of clinical AI studies
  • Hands-on imaging analysis and reproducible workflows
